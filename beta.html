Elecrow CrowPanel Advance 7.0 (DIS02170A, V1.2 to V1.5)

2026-08-27 · board: CrowPanel Advance 7.0, ESP32-S3 N16R8 (16 MB flash / 8 MB PSRAM), 7″ 800x480 RGB, GT911, STC8 backlight helper · base: 0.4.5

The Advance-series 7″ is a different animal from the classic CrowPanel 7.0: its own sync pins, I2C on 15/16, and the backlight is not a GPIO at all but a helper MCU (STC8 at I2C 0x30) that takes a single brightness byte. Pins and timings come from Elecrow's official ESPHome config for the V1.3/V1.4/V1.5 folder, cross-checked with an independent pinout database; their IDF sample in the same folder still carries copy-pasted Waveshare code and was deliberately not used. Full 16 MB class: OTA and the complete logo set included. Hardware V1.0 is not covered (it drove the backlight through a PCA9557); check the version print on the PCB - V1.2 and up is what this build expects.

CrowPanel Advance 7.0, 16 MB (0.4.5-crowpaneladv70) - first install: check Erase device

Command line: merged image, then esptool.py --chip esp32s3 --port PORT write_flash 0x0 esp32flight-0.4.5-crowpaneladv70-full.bin (back up first: read_flash 0 0x1000000 stock-backup.bin).

When reporting, include:

  • The version print on the board (V1.2 / V1.3 / V1.4 / V1.5).
  • Does the backlight come on and the picture show with correct colors (red is red)?
  • Does touch track your finger (not mirrored or offset)?
  • Does night mode dim the panel (that exercises the helper MCU path)?

Guition JC8012P4A1: the 10.1″ ESP32-P4 board - CONFIRMED WORKING

2026-08-29 · board: Guition JC8012P4A1 (ESP32-P4 + ESP32-C6, 10.1″ 800x1280 JD9365 MIPI-DSI, GSL3680 touch, 32 MB PSRAM / 16 MB flash) · base: 0.4.5

Panel V1 hardware-confirmed end to end (display, Wi-Fi over the C6, touch) - enormous thanks to Peter, who tested eleven rounds from Thailand and whose serial logs solved every layer of it. What it took, for the record: the two panel revisions need different init tables; the touch firmware in Guition's public demos is outdated (the factory image and the espcontrol project carry the real one); the Silead core needs the bring-up retried until its signature reads alive; and the touch reports a raw 1664x896 sensor grid with both axes inverted, which needs scaling, not axis flips. All of it is in the K4 build below.

Round K4 (2026-08-28), expected final: K1 proved the touch hardware works (the earlier "hardware fault" suspicion is withdrawn - the stock-image test was misled by the same problems our builds had). Peter's four-corner tap log then revealed the last surprise: the factory touch firmware reports in the controller's own sensor grid, 1664 x 896 units with both axes inverted - not in pixels, and not in any frame the references document. Mirror/swap flags can never fix that; it needs scaling. K4 converts the grid to pixels directly, and every point from the log lands where the finger was, corners within ~20 px.

Panel V1, K4 (0.4.5-jc10-v1-k4) - confirmed working, flash this one
Panel V1, touch K3 (0.4.5-jc10-v1-k3, flags-only mapping without grid scaling - by the corner-log math it should land off, kept for comparison)
Panel V1, touch K1 (0.4.5-jc10-v1-k1, superseded by K4, kept for reference)
Panel V1, touch K2 (0.4.5-jc10-v1-k2, superseded by K4, kept for reference)

Hardware sanity check, if both fail: flash Guition's stock image (JC8012P4A1_P4-V1.2.bin from their GitHub, esptool.py --chip esp32p4 write_flash 0x0 JC8012P4A1_P4-V1.2.bin) and see whether touch works there. If the factory firmware cannot use the touch either, it is the panel, not the software.

Panel V2 (0.4.5-jc10-v2-d, batch 2628 and higher, all K4 touch fixes included) - untested revision, reports welcome

Command line: V1 K4 / V1 K3 / V1 K1 / V1 K2 / V2 merged images, then esptool.py --chip esp32p4 --port PORT write_flash 0x0 <image> (back up first: read_flash 0 0x1000000 stock-backup.bin).

When reporting, include:

  • Your batch number and which build you flashed.
  • Does the picture come up, the right way round? If it is upside down, say so - a rotated build is one switch away.
  • K4: does the touch land exactly under your finger everywhere on the screen?
  • Does Wi-Fi connect (the C6 link is the other unknown on first hardware)?

7″ Amazon HMI boards: they are CrowPanel 7.0 rebrands

2026-08-11 · board: Elecrow CrowPanel 7.0 (DIS08070H) and unbranded 7″ rebrands (IoTeikXgo etc., EK9716BD3 driver, 4 MB flash) · base: 0.4.4

Plot twist confirmed by the listing's own support links: the no-name 7″ Amazon boards are rebranded Elecrow CrowPanel 7.0 units - not Suntons. Their PCLK sits on GPIO0 (like the CrowPanel 5.0 we already run) and the data bus differs from the Sunton 7″ too. This build carries Elecrow's official pin map and timings on the 4 MB class (updates via this flasher, no OTA; airline logos load on demand):

CrowPanel 7.0 / 7″ Amazon rebrands, 4 MB (0.4.4-crowpanel70) - first install: check Erase device
Genuine Sunton 8048S070, 16 MB (0.4.4-sunton70, v3 files) - owner-tuned 2026-08: R/B bus halves flipped (red rendered blue; the corrected bus matches the CrowPanel 7.0 wiring) and horizontal porches re-balanced to center the display window

When reporting, include:

  • Does the display come up, with correct colors (red is red, not blue)?
  • Does touch track your finger (not mirrored or offset)?
  • A photo of the PCB back (model silk print), if you can.

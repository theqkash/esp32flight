Everything a window seat wishes it knew

Point it at your Wi-Fi and it starts talking directly to open, free data networks. Your location and history never leave the device.

✈Live radar of your sky

Aircraft within 2–460 km from community ADS-B networks or your own dump1090 antenna. List, detail card, zoomable real-map radar, session stats and a phosphor retro CRT view.

🗺Who, where, whither

Airline with logo, route with cities and flags, progress bar, photo of the exact airframe, ETA in the destination's local time and a spotter line telling you where to look.

🌊More than planes

Optional layers, each one switch: rain radar, airspace zones, weather balloons, harbour ships with destinations, and the ISS when it crosses your sky.

🔔Alerts that find you

Emergency squawks, watchlist hits and „passes over you in ~3 min” pushed to your phone via ntfy.sh; webhooks for Node-RED and n8n; Home Assistant sensors over MQTT.

🖥A real web panel

Every setting in the browser, a live map, spotting history, Prometheus metrics and a documented JSON API with a full data-model reference.

🔧Honest engineering

Hackable C on ESP-IDF with an Android build of the same core for retired tablets. Aviation or metric units, English and Polish, 7 themes, OTA updates.

Screens

Straight off the device - 800×480 pixels of your local airspace.

Install in two minutes

Connect the board over USB, click Install, pick the serial port. Everything - firmware, airline logos, maps, airport database - is written in one go. Chrome or Edge on desktop required (Web Serial).

16 MB boards: Waveshare 7 and most others
8 MB boards: Waveshare 4.3 with the N8R8 module
Guition JC8048W550

Android instead? Grab esp32flight-v0.4.2.apk from the latest release. After the first flash, updates go over the air from the device's web panel.

Data it stands on

Community ADS-B (airplanes.live, adsb.lol, adsb.fi) with routes cross-checked against the aircraft's real position; maps by CARTO © OpenStreetMap contributors; rain by RainViewer; weather by Open-Meteo; METAR/TAF from aviationweather.gov; photos by planespotters.net; balloons by SondeHub; ISS by wheretheiss.at; ships by aisstream.io and airspace by openAIP (the last two with your own free keys). The full field-by-field reference lives in DATA-MODEL.md.
